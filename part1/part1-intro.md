# Part I - Principles of Security

This first part serves and an introductory part where we provide all the basic notions and terminology for security. There is not much novelty in this part, but it is an essential prerequisites to fully understand the remaining parts.

Experts in the area of security could skip this part entirely, as they would not learn much new. However, we suggest to get at least a skim in order to familiarize with the notation and the terminology adopted. Furthermore, also terms that are standard in the field, are sometimes used with slight different meanings. It is, therefore, important to understand the specific use that this book does of various terms.
